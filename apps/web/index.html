Built for sending, not guessing

A fare you agree

You make the offer and riders respond. Once you accept, the fare stays the same, even if your rider has to change route.

Real road distance

Fares start from the actual distance by road, not a straight line across the map.

Verified riders

Riders pass NIN, selfie, and driver’s licence checks, then staff review, before they can take a delivery.

Cash or card

Pay the rider in cash or by card. If a delivery goes wrong, you can report it from the trip for our team to review.
