Why we’re building FAST

A clearer way to send a parcel

Sending something across town should come with a few clear answers. What will it cost? Who’s picking it up? When does it arrive?

Those questions shaped FAST. We’re building a service where senders and riders agree on the fare up front, and both can follow the same delivery in the app. We’re starting with a pilot in Port Harcourt.

For customers

How delivery works

Set your route

Choose pickup and drop-off. See the road distance and minimum fare before making your offer.

Agree on a fare

Review offers from nearby riders and choose who takes your parcel. Pay by cash or card.

Follow your delivery

Track your rider on the map. Message or call through the app if you need to coordinate the handover.

For riders

Deliver when it suits you

Go available when you want to work. See parcels nearby, claim the offered fare, or send your own price.

  • You choose the jobs. See the fare, pickup distance, and trip length before you commit.
  • Help on the road. Get a new route if you miss a turn, and report a problem from any trip.
  • Sign up with your NIN and licence. Verification happens in the app before your first delivery.
  • Join as a rider
